Frequently asked questions

A helpful first stop.

If you cannot find what you need here, add your official support contact to the Resources page.

What is Greenwich?

Greenwich is a fictional Connecticut-inspired Roblox roleplay community. Build a character, explore town, meet other players, and make stories together.

How do I join the Roblox experience?

The Roblox experience link will be published here and in the community Discord when it is ready. Replace the marked Roblox links before launch.

Do I need Discord?

Discord is the best way to receive announcements, find events, and connect with the community. The experience itself should not depend on Discord.

How do I become staff or join a department?

Applications are not open yet. When they are, add the official application links to the Resources page.

How do I start a business?

Business participation is planned as part of the community experience. Check Resources for the official process once it is announced.

Where can I report a player or submit a suggestion?

The Resources page is the central place for reporting, appeals, bug reports, and suggestions. Add official form URLs there when they are available.
