About Greenwich

A place to make your own.

A fictional Connecticut-inspired roleplay community where every player can create a life, find their people, and help a town grow.

The experience

More than a map.

Greenwich is designed around the ordinary moments that make a roleplay community memorable: a first shift, a regular table at a local spot, a familiar face at an event, a neighborhood you know by heart.

There is no single way to belong here. Bring a character, create a career, start something new, or simply explore.
